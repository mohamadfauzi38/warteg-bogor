# Warteg Bogor

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, as explicitly requested for an empty project. The user selected a code-first build. The template must work by opening index.html and through a local HTTP server, without a build step or backend.

## Users

People in Bogor looking for Indonesian home-style meals, snacks, and drinks, who want to browse menu prices and arrange pickup or delivery through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

A reusable food-business website template: introduce the warteg, show eight example products, let visitors assemble an order, and prepare a WhatsApp message with customer details, fulfillment choice, quantities, and subtotal.

## Operating Context

Orders are arranged through WhatsApp. Delivery fees and availability are confirmed by the business. The site does not process payments or place orders automatically.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive navigation, product search, category filters, cart quantities and removal, and persistent localStorage cart.
- Checkout asks for a customer name, pickup or delivery, and a delivery address where appropriate.
- One editable config.js contains business identity, products, prices, photos, contact details, benefit copy, and optional example testimonials.
- The user approved Warteg Bogor and example data. Actual WhatsApp number, address, hours, and Instagram are unknown and must remain openly configurable. Do not direct orders to a invented phone number.
- Menu prices and testimonials are synthetic examples, visibly labeled. No unsupported halal, certification, customer rating, or performance claims.
- All page sections requested by the user must be implemented. Testimonials are optional and clearly example content.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Warteg Bogor. Indonesian copy with a friendly everyday voice. Main colors: warm cream, orange, and dark brown. The supplied screenshot is a reference for a clear local-food website with navigation, hero, benefits, and WhatsApp contact; its competitor logo and green palette are not adopted. The user explicitly requests research and a considered design without common AI-generated interface clichés.

## Evidence on Hand

One screenshot of a Warteg Kharisma Bahari page. No owner-supplied food photos, real business contact information, testimonials, certifications, or trading history. Licensed food imagery may illustrate the example menu and must retain source attribution; it is not evidence of this business's own premises or meals.

## Product Principles

- Make food, prices, and the next ordering step clear.
- Keep demo content honest and easy to replace.
- Give every control a working, understandable outcome.
- Keep the template editable without a framework or build tools.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, explicit form labels, visible focus, appropriate contrast, useful image alt text, reduced-motion support, and sufficiently large touch controls.
